The problem

Twelve tools. None of them talking.

The average mid-sized company runs finance in one system, support in another, and the truth about its customers in a spreadsheet someone updates on Fridays. Every answer takes three days and two apologies.

EQUIL isn't another box on that diagram. It's the layer underneath — one place where the numbers agree, the handoffs are automatic, and nobody has to ask which version is current.

Our approach

Three moves, in this order. It matters.

Automation before clean data creates fast mistakes. Dashboards without a feedback loop create expensive wallpaper. We sequence it deliberately.

01 — Connect

One record, every system

We map your CRM, ERP, billing and support into a single customer and order record. No migration, no rip-and-replace — your existing stack keeps running, it just stops disagreeing with itself.

02 — Automate

The boring work runs itself

Approvals, reconciliation, renewal notices and escalations move on their own. People are pulled in by exception, when judgement is actually needed — not to copy a number from one tab to another.

03 — Improve

A loop that keeps closing

Sentiment, revenue and throughput sit on one board with the same definitions. When a metric moves, EQUIL shows what moved it and what to do next — weekly, not quarterly.

The platform

Analytics & forecasting

Case study — Northpeak Logistics

From a five-day close to a next-morning one

Northpeak was growing 40% a year on a finance process held together by four spreadsheets and one very patient controller. Invoices were reconciled by hand, disputes surfaced weeks late, and nobody could say which customers were unhappy until they left.

In eleven days EQUIL connected their ERP, billing and support desk to a single order record, automated reconciliation, and wired Pulse into every delivery confirmation. The controller now reviews exceptions — 14 of them, instead of 2,000 line items.
