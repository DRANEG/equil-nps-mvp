We started EQUIL because we were the ones doing the reconciling.

Not consultants who read about the problem. Operators who spent too many Sundays making two systems agree before a Monday board meeting.

Our story

A spreadsheet called final_v7_REAL.xlsx

In 2019 our founders were running operations at a logistics company growing faster than its systems. Finance closed the month in five days. Support knew which customers were unhappy weeks before finance did, but had no way to say so that anyone would act on.

The fix everyone proposed was another tool. We'd already bought four. The actual problem was that none of them agreed on what a customer was — so every answer had to be assembled by hand, by someone, on a weekend.

EQUIL is the layer we wanted then: one record underneath the tools you already have, so the handoffs happen on their own and the numbers stop contradicting each other. That's it. That's the product.

Every company we meet has the same bottleneck, and it's never the one on the org chart. It's the twenty minutes someone spends every morning making two screens match. — Amara Osei, co-founder & CEO

Principles

Four things we won't trade away

These cost us deals occasionally. We've kept them anyway.

01

Fix the sequence, not the symptom

Automating a broken process just produces mistakes faster. We insist on connecting and cleaning first, even when it makes our first demo less impressive than the competitor's.

02

The customer owns their data

Full export, any time, in a format you can actually use. No lock-in through obscurity. If you leave, you leave with everything, and we'll help you move it.

03

Say the number out loud

Public pricing, honest timelines, and a straight answer when the tool isn't right for you. We've told companies to keep their spreadsheet. Some came back two years later.

04

Ship boring reliability

Nobody wants a thrilling data platform. We optimise for the days you never think about us — 99.97% uptime last year, and a status page that tells the truth in real time.

Timeline

Seven years, deliberately slow

2019

The weekend that started it

Two operators, one logistics company, and a month-end close that took five days and a weekend. The first version of EQUIL was a reconciliation script written to avoid a sixth.

2020

First eleven customers

All of them mid-sized, all of them in operations-heavy industries, all of them found by word of mouth. Nine are still with us.

2022

Pulse, and the closed loop

Customers kept asking why sentiment lived outside the system. We joined NPS to the same record as revenue and orders — and stopped calling it a survey tool.

2023

ISO 27001 and EU residency

Enterprise buyers needed proof, not promises. We got certified, opened Frankfurt, and published our security posture rather than gating it behind a sales call.

2026

3,200 businesses, 68 people

Still no outbound sales team. Still shipping the boring reliability. Pulse 2.0 turns sentiment into a weekly operating rhythm rather than a quarterly report card.
